# Build vs buy

Options for a real version. These are general categories. I have not evaluated specific vendors' current features or prices, and I make no claim about them.

| Option | Strength | Weakness | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRM-native validation (required fields, stage-exit rules) | Lives where reps work | Usually checks presence, not freshness or source, and does not handle narratives | First step in every case |
| Revenue intelligence or forecasting platform | Activity capture, roll-ups | Cost, a new system to adopt, may not encode a team's own gates | Larger teams with budget and a need for call capture |
| BI dashboard over the CRM | Flexible, cheap if BI exists | Logic spread across queries, no revision binding for narratives | Reporting-only needs |
| Small internal tool (this concept) | Exact rules, revision-bound narratives, auditable | Must be maintained, and integration work is real | When the gate rules are specific and the board narrative is the pain |

**My recommendation, conditional on discovery:** first enforce what the CRM can enforce natively. Build only the parts that are not commodity: revision-bound narratives, denominators and evidence-weighted forecast. Do a vendor scan before writing any code.
