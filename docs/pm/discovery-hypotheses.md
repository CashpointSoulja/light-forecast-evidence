# Discovery hypotheses and neutral interview guide

Status: **not tested**. No interviews have been conducted. Every hypothesis below is open.

## Hypotheses
| ID | Hypothesis | What would support it | What would disprove it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Forecast preparers spend meaningful time reconciling rep calls with evidence | They describe a recurring manual check | Evidence is already enforced in the CRM at stage change |
| H2 | Rep-called commit regularly includes deals missing economic-buyer engagement | Historical commit deals with no dated EB touch | EB engagement is reliably logged |
| H3 | Board narratives are reused after the underlying data changes | Examples of corrections after circulation | Narratives are generated live or always rebuilt |
| H4 | Unconverted or mixed currencies cause confusion in totals | Totals questioned in reviews | Single-currency book, or FX handled upstream |
| H5 | A "not classifiable" category would be acceptable to sales leaders | Leaders prefer visible gaps to optimistic categories | Leaders see it as noise or a reporting penalty |
| H6 | Owners act on named, sourced next actions | Gaps close within a week of being named | Actions are ignored without a manager follow-up |

## Neutral interview guide
I would use this with RevOps, sales leadership and deal owners. The questions are open and do not suggest the product.

1. Walk me through the last time you prepared a forecast. What did you open first?
2. When did you last change a deal's category during a review? What made you change it?
3. How do you know an economic buyer has been engaged? Where is that recorded?
4. Tell me about the last board or leadership pipeline update. How was it built? Was anything corrected after it was sent?
5. How are discounts and pricing approvals tracked on open deals?
6. How do you handle deals in other currencies when you report totals?
7. What would make you distrust a forecast number?
8. What happens after a review: how are follow-ups assigned and checked?
9. Is there anything about this process you would not want to change?

Rules: no leading questions, no product demo before the questions, ask for specific recent examples, record verbatim quotes with permission, and log disconfirming evidence with the same weight.
