# 5 Whys

This chain is a hypothesis I built from the public role description and common B2B forecasting patterns. It is not a finding about Light.

1. **Why might a board see a forecast it cannot trust?** The committed number can include deals the rep has called but the evidence does not support.
2. **Why would those deals be included?** Forecast category is often a rep judgement entered separately from the qualification evidence, so nothing forces them to agree.
3. **Why is the evidence not checked at the point of forecasting?** MEDDIC fields and economic-buyer engagement live in free-text fields or notes, without dates or source references, so nobody can check them quickly.
4. **Why are they unchecked when the board pack is written?** The narrative is usually written from a snapshot and copied forward. Nobody marks it out of date when deals change later.
5. **Why does that persist?** Nothing links the narrative to a specific data revision, and nothing reports how much of the pipeline could be classified at all.

**Root cause I am designing against:** forecast categories, evidence and narratives are not tied to the same revision with explicit denominators. The workbench ties them together: evidence gates the category, totals show their denominators, and narratives carry a revision.
