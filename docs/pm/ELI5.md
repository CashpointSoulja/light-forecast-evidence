# ELI5

Imagine a class guessing how many sweets are in a jar. Everyone says a number, but the teacher only writes a guess on the board if the person can point to how they worked it out.

This workbench does that for sales deals. A salesperson might say "this deal will close". The workbench checks whether the reasons are written down and can be pointed to: who has the budget, when we last spoke to them, whether the price was approved, what happens next. If something is missing, the deal does not get counted as a sure thing, and the screen says exactly what is missing and who should fix it.

When the facts change, any old summary for the board gets a big "out of date" label, so nobody reads the old story by mistake.

All the deals in it are made up.
