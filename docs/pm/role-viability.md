# Role viability

How this concept maps to the public posting, and where it does not. This is not a claim about the role's day-to-day work, Light's internal state, or any application outcome.

**Covered by the concept:** opportunity inspection, forecasting discipline, board-pack preparation, MEDDIC and stage gates, economic-buyer engagement, pricing approvals, weekly review inputs, measurable adoption framing, and connected workflow design (as a design, not live).

**Not covered:** account research and meeting intelligence agents, follow-up automation, territories, capacity and quota planning, lead routing, attribution, partner performance, onboarding, playbooks, and the full Lead → Close → Renew → Expand lifecycle.

**What it shows about how I work:** I start from evidence and denominators, I refuse to invent baselines, I put guardrails around automation, and I test everything I claim.

**What it cannot show:** stakeholder management, real CRM administration, and outcomes on live pipeline.
