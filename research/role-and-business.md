# Research: Light and the AI & Revenue Operations Lead role

I'm Ayo Ahmed. This is my research for an independent concept, the Forecast Evidence Workbench. It is not an official Light product, and I have no affiliation with, employment at or internal access to Light. Everything below comes from Light's public pages, read on 2026-10-13. Statements about pain points are my hypotheses, not problems I found inside Light.

## The business (public sources)
- Light describes itself as "Agentic ERP for multi-entity companies": one ledger for every entity, plus apps and AI agents that "do the work" while "you set policy and approve" (light.inc home page).
- It has three product layers: Light ledger ("Knows the business"), Light apps ("Adapts to the business", 600+ apps in the App Store, CRM among them) and Light agents ("Operates the business"). Agents work inside written policy, and "every action is logged to the agent that took it".
- It is positioned against NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Sage Intacct, SAP and per-entity Xero/QuickBooks, plus card, bill-pay, subscription-billing and consolidation tools.
- Customer stories on the public site: Famly, Tillo (4 entities, 23 currencies), Ocean.io, All Gravy, Officeguru. I cite these only as Light's own public claims. None of their data is used here.
- Partners: KPMG (announced 2026-09-08, "140+ countries"), J.P. Morgan, BDO, Cooper Parry, Greenstep (Light blog).
- Careers page: 110+ team members; offices in London, Copenhagen and Stockholm; 16 open roles, 10 of them GTM. The legal entity in the Terms of Service is Light Company ApS, Copenhagen.
- The public site loads HubSpot (the cookie banner and comments in its page source name HubSpot), and the role names HubSpot as the CRM. I have no HubSpot access, and this concept does not connect to HubSpot.

## The exact role
Source: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/light-inc/c8970651-2f80-442d-9c5a-348c845cb116. Operations team, London, on-site, full time, published 2026-09-30.

The parts of the role this concept speaks to (quoted from the posting):
- "Build reliable AI agents and connected workflows for account research, meeting intelligence, opportunity inspection, follow-ups, forecasting and reporting … evaluate accuracy and measure the time saved and commercial impact."
- "Own a trusted view of pipeline, conversion, win rates … Help the VP Sales and Finance prepare the commercial board pack, translating performance and forecasts into a clear narrative and recommendations."
- "Embed MEDDIC, stage gates, economic buyer engagement, pricing approvals and forecast discipline."
- 60 days: "deliver a trusted performance view, strengthen the forecast cadence and put the first priority AI workflows into use."
- 90 days: "demonstrate adoption and measurable improvement."
- "Bring an example of a system, automation or commercial initiative you owned: the problem, what you built, how you brought people with you and what changed."

Parts of the role this concept does **not** cover: territories, capacity and quota planning; lead routing and attribution; partner performance; onboarding and the sales playbook; the full Lead → Close → Renew → Expand handoffs. These are listed as fit gaps in `docs/role-viability.md`.

## What I infer (hypotheses, TO TEST)
- H1: A commercial board pack takes in pipeline numbers, but the evidence behind each number (economic buyer, pricing sign-off, a live next step) is not visible in the same view. *Basis: the posting asks for both "trusted view" and "forecast discipline". No internal evidence.*
- H2: In a multi-currency business, gross pipeline, weighted forecast and closed-won can be quoted on different FX assumptions or different row sets unless the denominators are shown. *Basis: Light sells to multi-entity, multi-currency companies, and its own GTM likely spans GBP, EUR, DKK, SEK and USD. Assumption, not observed.*
- H3: A narrative written from an earlier snapshot can survive into a later meeting after the numbers have changed. *Basis: a common forecast-cadence failure mode. Not observed at Light.*
