Adoption hypothesis (not a result)

If forecast calls show the evidence gap next to the number, I expect managers to ask for the missing evidence before the call rather than during it. This is untested.

  • Baseline: unknown. I have no data on how forecast calls run today, so there is no time-saved or accuracy-lift figure here.
  • Leading measure: share of open deals classifiable, with the count of open deals as the denominator.
  • Usage measure: weekly reviews where a narrative was generated from the current revision, out of weekly reviews held.
  • Guardrail: rep calls ahead of evidence, out of open deals with a rep call. It should fall, not be hidden.
  • Kill criterion: after a four-week pilot, if managers still rebuild the number in a spreadsheet for most reviews, the tool is not earning its place.

About this concept

I'm Ayo Ahmed. I built this as an independent concept around the public AI & Revenue Operations Lead role at Light. It is not a Light product. I have no affiliation with Light and no access to its systems, customers or data.

The Light name and logo appear only as context for which public role this responds to. They belong to Light and are used here without endorsement.

Everything in the workbench is synthetic: companies, people, amounts, references and FX rates. Nothing reads from or writes to a CRM, ERP, email or finance system. Each browser gets its own isolated workspace, stored in a SQLite-backed Durable Object.

The PRD, research, rules, test results and caveats are in the docs/ folder of the public repository.
